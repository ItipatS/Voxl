# 01 — Vision

## Pitch

A cozy 3D voxel world where you **own a star**, gather from **biome-flavored public worlds**, and build the thing you actually want to build — with a living but optional economy underneath, powered by the fact that no single biome has everything.

## What it IS

- A **builder-gatherer** first. Building your own space is the point; everything else serves it.
- A **shared world** with many small owned worlds, browsable in a social lobby.
- An **opt-in economy**: trade is convenient, self-sufficiency is always possible.
- **Specialization-friendly**: you can be a gatherer, a builder, a crafter, a trader, a factory-optimizer, and all of it plugs into one system.

## What it is NOT

- **Not infinite per-player worlds.** Infinite space inside infinitely many worlds is absurd and unownable. Worlds are **bounded** (see doc 06). Only *public resource stars* are the shared "wild" for gathering.
- **Not Cubic Castles' frozen grind.** We change/tune continuously; the economy is not the whole point.
- **Not Islands' solo farm-treadmill.** We avoid "gather → sell → unlock a faster way to gather → repeat" as the *only* loop. Progression is largely **horizontal** (new ways to express/build), not just a bigger number.
- **Not Albion's full-loot PvP.** We steal Albion's economic *geography* (regional markets, weight-as-friction), not its death-and-loss psychology.
- **Not timer-crafting.** Waiting hours for a rare item is a dark pattern. We replace it with factory *management* (doc 04).
- **Not a UGC item-authoring platform** (unless explicitly decided later — see OPEN in doc 09). Players craft *instances* of dev-defined item types; they do not design new item art.

## Target feel

- **Minute 1:** mining a block and placing one feels tactile and satisfying with zero goals. (This is the make-or-break; see doc 08.)
- **Hour 1:** "oh, my biome is great for wood but I have no fiber — I can gather it slowly, or find someone who has it."
- **Day 30:** "my star is becoming something; I have a backpack that lets me haul real loads; I know which biomes I trade with."
- **Never:** "the optimal move is to log off and let idle do everything," or "I have to buy my way past content I never played."

## Strategic bet

Standalone voxel-economy games (Cubic Castles) capped out small because they had to win distribution alone. Voxl is on **Roblox** — it rides the platform's installed base. The prize is not novelty (this genre shape is proven) — it is being the one that finally makes the loop **not feel empty**, by fixing the documented failures of the games it descends from.

## The one-sentence reason to log in

**"To make my star a little more mine."** Trade, factories, skills, and social all exist to serve that sentence.
